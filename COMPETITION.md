## 2. What we must build

An AI solution that turns synthetic, football-realistic match events into explainable match
intelligence, real-time narratives and automated recaps for studio, broadcast or streaming, and
lets fans in any market tailor the experience.

**One pipeline, five stages:** ingest → interpret → explain → render → personalize.

Named features to consider: player name tags with speed and distance alerts at thresholds; pass
distance, accuracy and difficulty; ball and shot speed; auto-eventing; key narratives, milestones
and recaps; explaining control vs. chaos, pressure changes and game rhythm; creating or extending
synthetic datasets; multilingual output.
